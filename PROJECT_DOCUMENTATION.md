# Prison Escape — Game Documentation

## Game Title
Prison Escape

## Game Description
Prison Escape is a 2D arcade adventure in which the player guides a female prisoner through a guarded facility. The player must collect a key, cross a timed laser-security area, obtain an access card and reach the exit door. A moving guard and timed laser beams make the escape more challenging.

## Game Genre
2D Arcade / Adventure / Puzzle.

## Gameplay
The player moves the prisoner using the arrow keys or W/A/S/D. First, the player collects the yellow key. This unlocks the laser-security challenge and reveals the access card. The player must observe the laser timing and cross while the beams are inactive. After collecting the access card, the exit becomes available. Contact with the guard or an active laser returns the player to a checkpoint.

## Motivations for Playing
The game encourages players to complete a sequence of clear objectives, observe obstacle patterns, time their movement and improve their attempts. The combination of patrol movement and timed laser barriers provides a simple challenge with a clear goal.

## Target Audience
Casual players and students interested in 2D arcade games, puzzle challenges and introductory game-development concepts.

## Controls
- Arrow keys or W/A/S/D: move.
- Click the start/replay button: begin or restart the game.
